• Daily rewards that grow bigger every day for 7 days in a row

⬆️ UPGRADES
Boost the power of all your pets and hatch higher-level pets right from the start.

✨ FEATURES
• Simple one-finger controls — play anywhere, anytime
• Cute, colorful look with smooth animations
• Satisfying merge effects, sounds and vibration (can be turned off)
• Works offline — no internet needed
• No ads, no sign-up, no personal data collected
• Available in English and Russian
