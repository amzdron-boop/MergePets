Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Merge Pets ("the app") is a game developed by Software Kasper Team.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store on our servers, transmit or share any personal information. The app does not require an account and does not use the internet.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, pets, upgrades and settings such as sound, vibration and language) is saved only locally on your device. It is not sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Permissions

The app does not request any special permissions.

Third-party services

The app does not contain advertising, analytics or any other third-party services.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions? Write to GitHub Issues.
